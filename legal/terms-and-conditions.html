2. Ordering

You may order through this website during kitchen hours: 12:00 PM to 11:00 PM, Mondays to Sundays. Prices on the menu are in Ghana cedis (GHS). The bag total you see before you send the order is the amount we cook against, unless we agree a change with you by phone.

You may pay on delivery, or pay now with mobile money. Cooking for a mobile-money order starts after the payment is confirmed. A pay-on-delivery order is a promise to pay the rider or kitchen the bag total when the food arrives.

4. Food, notes and allergies

Tell us about allergies, spice, or a delivery landmark in the notes. Photographs on the site are a guide to the plate, not a guarantee of garnish or portion shape. We cook to order. Once rice, meat or pastry is on the fire we cannot reverse it.

5. Delivery, pickup and events

Delivery times are estimates. Traffic in Accra can move a window. Arranged pickup is only when we have agreed it. Private events at the hub need a booking. Without a booking the hub is a delivery kitchen, not a public dining room.

6. When we may refuse or delay

We may refuse or delay an order if the kitchen is full, an item has run out, the address is outside the area we can reach that hour, or the order looks unsafe or not genuine. If we cannot fulfil an order we have accepted, we will call or email you and refund any mobile-money payment we have taken for that order.

Cooked food is covered by our Return Policy, published as a separate PDF on this website. Read it before you order.
